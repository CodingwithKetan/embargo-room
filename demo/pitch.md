# 45-second pitch (video open + close)

The first and last 15 seconds decide how a judge remembers the project. Memorize
these two; everything between is the demo in `demo/narration.md`.

## Open (0:00–0:20) — the hook

> "In security, the patch almost always exists. What breaks is the *choreography*
> — between a researcher and a vendor who don't trust each other, racing an
> embargo clock. And the fix the vendor ships? It often doesn't actually fix the
> bug. Embargo Room runs that entire process inside **one Switch room**: AI agents
> keep the clock, reproduce the bug, and verify the vendor's real code — while the
> two decisions that must stay human, stay human."

## Close (last 20s) — the landing

> "Everything you saw is real and reproducible from a clean clone — real CVEs, real
> Docker builds, a tamper-evident record, and a disclosure scorecard that grades
> the whole thing against its SLA. Remove Switch and this is disconnected bots and
> separate inboxes. *As a Switch room*, it's a disclosure desk that a security team
> could run tomorrow. That's Embargo Room."

## The one line, if you only get one

> "Coordinated disclosure is mutually distrusting parties sharing one timeline while
> agents act on it — which is exactly what a Switch room is, and what disconnected
> bots can never be."
