Coordinated vulnerability disclosure

Embargo Room

One Switch room runs a disclosure from report to published advisory. Agents verify and keep the clock; humans hold the two decisions that must stay human. Remove the shared room and it's disconnected bots and separate inboxes.

🛡️ Don't trust the vendor's word

The agent builds the vendor's actual commit and re-runs the exploit — two independent sources (the build, and the OSV record) must agree.
